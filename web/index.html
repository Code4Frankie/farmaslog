How FarmasLog works

Banks, programmes and government agencies get a quick, honest check on a smallholder record. Behind every check is a record that people on the ground have registered, reviewed and visited.

For banks, programmes and agencies

  1. Request access. Register your organisation. A FarmasLog administrator reviews and approves it before sign-in works.
  2. Get a test key. Create a test API key from your dashboard, up to five at a time. Live keys are issued by FarmasLog.
  3. Check a farmer. Enter a phone number or FarmasLog ID on the Verify screen, or call the API from your own system. You see whether a record exists, where the farm is, the crop, and how strong the evidence is.
  4. Track your use. The Usage tab shows requests and billable checks by month. Only successful lookups are billed.

How a farmer gets on the record

  1. Registered with consent. A field agent or administrator records the farmer, the farm and its location, after the farmer agrees to what is collected and why.
  2. Reviewed. A data manager approves the record, or flags it if it looks like a duplicate.
  3. Visited. A field agent visits the farm and confirms its location with GPS.
  4. Evidence grows. Every record shows how it is backed: self-reported, satellite signal, or verified in the field. Lenders see that level with each result.
